Clarity before scale.

Revenue Architecture connects the decisions that determine whether a strong product becomes a repeatable commercial system.

They’ll call it a technical problem. It’s often a go-to-market alignment problem.

The founder problem

Your GTM functions may be working. They may not be working together.

Revenue stalls when Product, Sales, Marketing, and Partnerships operate from different assumptions about the market. Each team can execute its own work well while the company still fails to create a coherent path from product value to revenue.

  • Product stories shift by team, audience, or sales call.
  • The ICP expands without a controlled market decision.
  • Launches create attention without durable pipeline.
  • Partnerships exist without an operating revenue motion.

Advisory triggers

When to bring me in.

The best time is before disconnected decisions harden into process, headcount, and missed revenue.

  • Your product works, but buyers do not understand why it matters.
  • Product, Sales, and Marketing describe the company differently.
  • You are moving upmarket without an enterprise-ready narrative.
  • Launches create attention but do not produce durable pipeline.
  • Partnerships exist but are not generating measurable revenue.
  • You need senior PMM leadership before making a full-time executive hire.
  • Your team is scaling execution before agreeing on how the company wins.
