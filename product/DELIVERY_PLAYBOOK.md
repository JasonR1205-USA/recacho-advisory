# Revenue Architecture Diagnostic Delivery Playbook

## Stage 0 — Qualification

1. Review the website application within two business days.
2. Confirm:
   - one decision is due within 60 days;
   - the applicant is the decision owner or can bring that person;
   - the company has customers, pipeline, or other credible market evidence;
   - the decision fits the diagnostic rather than a broader implementation sprint.
3. Decline, refer, or rescope when these conditions are not met.
4. Confirm the engagement fee in writing, then send the short agreement and invoice only after fit is confirmed.

## Stage 1 — Secure intake

Before files are shared, agree in writing on:

- the secure exchange method;
- who may access the evidence;
- prohibited data such as passwords, credentials, payment details, and unnecessary customer personal information;
- deletion timing;
- whether interviews or recordings are included.

Collect no more than eight artifacts. Prefer the smallest set that answers the decision:

1. Current website or product narrative.
2. Sales deck or demo narrative.
3. Pipeline or opportunity summary.
4. Three to five representative customer or prospect records.
5. Win/loss evidence.
6. Product or usage evidence.
7. Resource or headcount plan.
8. Partner or ecosystem evidence when relevant.

## Stage 2 — Kickoff

Run a 45-minute kickoff.

Required outputs:

- one-sentence decision;
- named decision owner;
- deadline;
- alternatives under consideration;
- constraints;
- success condition;
- cost of error;
- known evidence gaps.

Pause the engagement when leadership expands the scope to several unrelated decisions.

## Stage 3 — Evidence review

1. Create the evidence register in `DIAGNOSTIC_RUBRIC.md`.
2. Review each source across the four Revenue Architecture layers.
3. Separate supported facts, reasonable inferences, hypotheses, and important unknowns.
4. Build the contradiction map.
5. Identify the highest-materiality contradiction affecting the stated decision.
6. Request clarification only when an unanswered question could change the recommendation.

AI policy:

- Tools may organize, summarize, or compare source material.
- Do not upload client material to a system that conflicts with the agreed data boundary.
- Verify all AI-assisted statements against the original source.
- Jason owns every conclusion.

## Stage 4 — Decision memo

Use `DECISION_MEMO_TEMPLATE.md`.

The deliverable must include:

- executive diagnosis;
- recommended call;
- confidence and unresolved evidence;
- evidence map;
- contradiction map;
- 30-day plan;
- “do not do yet” guidance;
- decision gate.

Complete the quality gate in `DIAGNOSTIC_RUBRIC.md` before scheduling the readout.

## Stage 5 — Leadership readout

Run a 60-minute session:

1. Restate the decision and constraints.
2. Present the diagnosis before supporting detail.
3. Walk through the two or three decisive contradictions.
4. Review evidence that weakens the recommendation.
5. Confirm the decision owner’s response.
6. Assign owners for the 30-day plan.
7. Record the decision gate and follow-up date.

Do not convert disagreement into artificial consensus. Record unresolved executive assumptions explicitly.

## Stage 6 — Follow-up and learning

At approximately 30 days:

- record whether the recommended decision was adopted;
- record actions completed;
- capture observable pipeline, conversion, buyer, narrative, or partner changes;
- distinguish workflow outcomes from causal revenue claims;
- ask whether the diagnostic was worth the fixed fee;
- identify whether a sprint is justified.

Record the result in `outcome-tracker.csv`.

## Operating service levels

| Event | Service level |
| --- | --- |
| Application response | Within two business days |
| Fit confirmation and invoice | After qualification |
| Kickoff | After payment |
| Evidence-complete acknowledgment | Within one business day |
| Decision memo | Within five business days of complete inputs |
| Readout | Included in the five-day delivery plan |
| Outcome review | Approximately 30 days after readout |

Missing inputs pause delivery. Scope changes require a new agreement or a move to the Revenue Architecture Sprint.
