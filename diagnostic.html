02

Evidence, not a founder questionnaire alone.

The inputs are deliberately small enough to review deeply and broad enough to expose cross-functional conflict.

Market evidence

Where can the company win?

Current ICP, customer interviews, win/loss patterns, pipeline mix, competitive context, and market constraints.

Narrative evidence

Why should the buyer care?

Website, pitch, demo story, sales materials, product claims, customer proof, and objections.

Revenue-motion evidence

How does demand become revenue?

Funnel stages, sales process, deal friction, handoffs, launch behavior, and resource allocation.

Ecosystem evidence

Where can leverage compound?

Technology dependencies, integrations, partners, co-sell motions, channels, and platform relationships.

Evidence boundary

Up to eight relevant artifacts are included. Do not send passwords, customer personal data, raw credentials, or confidential files with the initial application. A secure exchange method is agreed after fit is confirmed.

What you receive

A decision memo your leadership team can act on.

Every conclusion is connected to available evidence and labeled by confidence. Unknowns stay visible instead of being converted into false certainty.

03

A narrow fit by design.

The diagnostic is most useful when the product is real, the decision is imminent, and leadership is prepared to act on evidence.

Strong fit

Use the diagnostic when:

  • You lead a seed-to-Series B AI, data, or SaaS company.
  • A consequential GTM decision is due within 60 days.
  • The product has customers, active pipeline, or credible market evidence.
  • Product, Sales, Marketing, or Partnerships are operating from different assumptions.
  • The decision owner will participate in the kickoff and readout.
Not the right product

Do not use it for:

  • A generic marketing audit or maturity score.
  • Campaign production, lead generation, or sales outsourcing.
  • A company without a product or meaningful market evidence.
  • A broad transformation covering several unrelated decisions.
  • Validation of a decision leadership has already made and will not revisit.

04

Operating terms.

A compact engagement with explicit boundaries.

When does the five-day clock start?

After payment, the kickoff, and receipt of the agreed evidence set. Missing inputs pause delivery rather than forcing unsupported conclusions.

How is confidential information handled?

No confidential files are submitted through this page. A secure exchange method, access scope, and deletion timing are agreed before evidence is shared.

Is this AI-generated advice?

No. Tools may assist organization and analysis, but Jason reviews the source evidence, owns every conclusion, and keeps confidence and unknowns visible.

What if the diagnostic is not the right fit?

You will receive a direct response before any agreement or invoice. A broader sprint or a different specialist may be the better answer.
